1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Multiply Or Release: Marble TD game application and to privacy-related information presented through the game and any official associated pages.

In this policy, "Multiply Or Release: Marble TD," "the game," "we," "us," and "our" refer to the developer and publisher of the Multiply Or Release: Marble TD application.

2. Information handled by Multiply Or Release: Marble TD

Multiply Or Release: Marble TD creates and manages information such as the following to provide its single-player features:

Type of information Purpose Where it is stored
Current run progress (battle number, shields, gold, upgrades) To let you continue an eight-battle Roguelite run across sessions. Locally on your device.
Machine configurations (machine style, tower wheel, army loadout) To remember how you programmed your war slot machine before each battle. Locally on your device.
Unlocked content skeleton To record which future options remain available to unlock. Locally on your device.
Save version and current language To keep your save file compatible and to display the game in your preferred language. Locally on your device.
Game preferences (vibration, reduced motion) To respect your accessibility and comfort settings during play. Locally on your device.
Legacy left/right-hand and old gold/upgrade fields To remain compatible with older save files; these fields are read-only and no longer affect battle layout. Locally on your device.

This information is used only to provide the game's local features. Multiply Or Release: Marble TD does not operate a server that receives or stores this content.

4. Local storage and retention

Information created in Multiply Or Release: Marble TD is stored locally on your device. We cannot view, retrieve, recover, or remotely delete this information because it is not uploaded to us.

Local information remains on your device until you delete it within the game, clear the game's storage through your device settings, or uninstall the game.

Device backups may include application data depending on your operating-system and backup settings. Those backups are controlled by you and the relevant platform provider.

5. Runs, saves, and checkpoints

A complete run consists of eight battles. Your remaining shields and their durability carry over between battles, so each Multiply decision has consequences across the whole run.

The game saves your current run number, gold, shields, tower upgrades, machine configuration, and input preferences. It does not save Phaser sprites, physics bodies, tweens, or scene references, and it does not perform frame-by-frame saves during a battle.

If you refresh the page, close the app, or the process is terminated during a battle, the next launch returns you to the machine-programming checkpoint before that battle. Your run progress, gold, shields, tower upgrades, machine configuration, and input preferences are preserved, and the reason for recovery is shown to you.

Soldiers, towers, batch queues, and temporary deployment state inside a single battle are not carried over to the next battle.

6. Game settings

Multiply Or Release: Marble TD stores a small number of player preferences locally, including:

  • Vibration on or off.
  • Reduced motion for shorter reel animations.
  • Current display language.

These preferences affect only how the game looks and feels on your device. They are not shared with us or with any third party.

7. Offline play

Multiply Or Release: Marble TD is designed as an offline-first, premium single-player game. After installation, a complete eight-battle run can be started, paused, and finished without an internet connection.

The game does not require network access for gameplay, saves, settings, runs, or unlocks. No background data transfer occurs while you play.

8. Multilingual support

The first release of Multiply Or Release: Marble TD ships with Simplified Chinese. The architecture is prepared for additional languages, and the language selection entry point will appear once a second language is added.

Your language preference is stored locally on your device and is used only to display interface text in your preferred language.

9. Deleting your data

You can use the game's reset or "Delete all data" function, where available, to remove all locally stored Multiply Or Release: Marble TD content and preferences.

You may also delete local game data through your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Because we do not maintain user accounts or a remote database of game content, there is no server-side account or content deletion request to submit to us.
